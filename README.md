# Law of Entropy

**Article 1.** All things tend toward disorder. The Departement maintains
order only through deliberate, continuous effort.

**Article 2.** Cleanup is a civic duty. Desks, repos, and plans left
untouched for thirty days may be lawfully composted.

**Article 3.** Negentropy — the local reversal of disorder — is the highest
form of productivity and shall be rewarded accordingly.
